Leaving Homesick

Account and data deletion

You may request deletion of your Homesick account and associated personal data at any time.

Request deletion online

Send an email from the address connected to your Homesick account. Use the subject line Delete my Homesick account and include the email address used to sign in.

Request account deletion

Delete from inside the app

The production version of Homesick must also provide a clear in-app deletion path:

Settings → Account → Delete account

Before publishing on Google Play, confirm that this option exists and completes deletion rather than only signing the user out.

What will be deleted

  • your authentication account;
  • your user profile and settings;
  • private letters and drafts stored only in your account;
  • people/connection records and time capsules;
  • photos and files in your private Firebase Storage folders; and
  • other personal data directly associated with your account, unless retention is legally required.

What may remain

A copy of a letter that you deliberately delivered to another user may remain in that recipient's account as part of their own correspondence. Limited records may also be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance or dispute resolution.

How long it takes

We aim to verify and complete valid deletion requests within 30 days. We may contact you to confirm account ownership.
