Your Privacy

Privacy Policy · Effective 21 September 2026

Homesick was created with a simple belief: some words deserve to last. Protecting the personal information connected to those words is part of that promise.

The words you write belong to you. Homesick does not claim ownership of your letters, photographs or memories. We store and process them only so the service can work and your chosen recipients can receive them.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy explains how Homesick, operated by an independent developer, handles information when you use the Homesick mobile application and related support pages.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you create or use an account, we may receive your name, email address, user identifier and profile image from the sign-in method you choose, including Google Sign-In or email authentication.

Content you create

We store content you choose to create or upload, including letters, time capsules, connection information, photographs, postcards, family events, shared tasks, family updates and metadata such as creation dates, delivery status and intended recipients.

Technical and advertising information

Firebase and Google services may process limited technical information needed to authenticate users, maintain security, operate cloud storage and deliver the service. Where advertising is enabled, Google Mobile Ads may process device identifiers, approximate location derived from an IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic information to provide, measure and protect advertisements. Available consent and advertising choices depend on your device, account and region.

3. Why we use information

  • create and secure your account;
  • save, display and deliver the letters and memories you choose to create;
  • connect you with recipients you select;
  • store photographs and other user-provided content;
  • provide support, prevent misuse and maintain security; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Service providers

Homesick uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage, and may use Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. Google processes information to provide these services under its own privacy terms. Google Sign-In may also process information according to your Google account settings and policies.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with Google to display and measure advertisements, with the people you intentionally include in a Homesick connection or family space, with service providers needed to operate Homesick, or where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect users and the service. Letters, family updates and other private content are not provided to advertisers for ad targeting.

6. Storage and international processing

Information may be stored and processed using Google Firebase infrastructure. Depending on your location and service configuration, data may be processed outside your country. We use reasonable safeguards and Firebase security rules to restrict access.

7. Data security

Homesick uses authenticated access, access-control rules and encrypted network connections provided by Firebase and modern mobile platforms. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure, but we work to limit access and address known security issues.

8. Data retention

We retain account information and user content while your account remains active or while needed to provide the service. When you delete your account, we aim to remove associated account data within 30 days, except where limited information must be retained for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance or dispute resolution.

Letters already delivered to another user may remain in that recipient's account as part of their own correspondence, unless removal is technically and legally appropriate.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, including under South Africa's POPIA and the European Union's GDPR, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your personal information, and to object to certain processing.

We may update this policy as Homesick develops or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated, and material changes may also be communicated in the app.
