Your Privacy

Privacy Policy · Updated 29 September 2026

This policy explains how Homesick handles information when you use the mobile app and its support pages. Homesick is operated by Gabriela González Segura, an independent developer in South Africa. You can contact us at gabgonse1310@gmail.com.

Your words remain yours. You own the letters, photographs and memories you create. Homesick processes them to provide the features you choose to use, including delivery to the people you select.

1. Information we handle

Account and profile

We handle your email address, name, account identifier, verification status, profile details and settings to create and secure your account. Firebase Authentication validates sign-in credentials; passwords are not stored in Homesick's own database.

Letters, memories and people

We store letters, drafts, scanned pages, photographs, keepsakes, time capsules, intended recipients, delivery status and related dates that you choose to create or upload. Some content is also stored on your device.

Family Hub and birthdays

We store the people you add, invitations, family circles, event details, tasks, updates, participant selections, birthdays, gift ideas and reminders you create. Other members of a family circle may see information you share with that circle. Recipients may keep copies of letters you deliver to them.

Notifications and calendar

If you enable notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging may process a device token and delivery information so Homesick can send alerts. If you choose to add an event or birthday to a device calendar, the app requests calendar access and writes the event to the calendar you select. That calendar may sync through its provider under your own calendar settings.

Purchases

Apple App Store or Google Play processes subscriptions and payments. Homesick receives purchase and entitlement information needed to provide Premium features. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Advertising and technical data

Homesick uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display ads in the free version. The ads SDK may collect and share IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information and device or account identifiers, including an advertising identifier where available, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. IP address can indicate an approximate location. The data and ads you see can depend on your device settings, region and applicable choices. We do not send the text of your letters to AdMob for ad targeting.

Firebase and device platforms also process technical data needed for authentication, security, storage, app operation and error handling.

2. How we use information

  • create, verify and protect accounts;
  • save and deliver letters and memories to recipients you select;
  • provide Family Hub, birthdays, reminders, calendars and Premium features;
  • display ads and measure their operation in the free version;
  • respond to support requests, reports and account deletion requests; and
  • prevent abuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Who receives information

Content you choose to share is available to its selected recipients or family participants. Service providers process information needed to run Homesick: Google Firebase for authentication, databases, file storage, Cloud Functions and messaging; Google AdMob for advertising; and Apple or Google for in-app purchases. These providers may process information under their own policies. We do not sell the content of your letters or family updates.

We may disclose limited information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users and the service. The Google Privacy Policy explains Google's handling of information, and your app store explains its purchase data practices.

4. Storage, security and retention

Homesick uses Firebase infrastructure, which may process data outside your country. We use authenticated access, access rules and encrypted network connections to help protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

We keep account data and content while your account is active or while needed to provide the service. The in-app Delete Account flow is designed to remove your account, private records and private uploads. A delivered letter and any media needed to view it may remain with its recipient as part of their correspondence. Limited reports or records may be retained where needed for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance or disputes. Copies in provider backups may remain for a limited period under those providers' retention practices.

Deleting your Homesick account does not automatically cancel a subscription managed by Apple or Google. Cancel it separately in your App Store or Google Play account.

5. Your choices and rights

You can decide what content to create and whom to include in a letter or family activity. You can manage notification and calendar permissions in device settings and advertising identifiers through your device's privacy settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your personal information. Contact us for assistance.

Homesick is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where local law requires it. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information without appropriate consent.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app or legal requirements change. The update date above will change, and material changes may also be communicated in the app. For privacy questions or requests, email gabgonse1310@gmail.com.
